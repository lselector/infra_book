> _Honeypot seems to be less effective in forms to prevent spam submissions. So, we suggest you add a proper captcha to protect your form._
> Bots and Spam Submissions are prevented using the Honeypot Spam Prevention method. By now, these bots are getting advanced, so we have made sure to add some extra layer of protection for this Honeypot. This will stop most bots from submitting your form.
> Honeypot is optional to include, however we recommend adding this if you are not using the [hCaptcha](https://docs.web3forms.com/getting-started/customizations/spam-protection/hcaptcha) or [reCaptcha Integration](https://docs.web3forms.com/getting-started/pro-features/recaptcha-integration)
