# Drata

Continuous control monitoring and evidence collection for
[[SOC 2]], ISO 27001 and other frameworks, in the same
category as [[Vanta]].

## How to choose between them

They are close substitutes. Compare on:

- **Integrations** with the specific stack you run — this
  is the practical differentiator.
- **Auditor network**, if you want the platform to
  introduce one.
- **Framework coverage**, if ISO 27001 or HIPAA is also
  in view.
- Price at your headcount.

## The advice that applies to both

Do not buy either before you know you need the audit.
Implement the controls first — they are worth having
regardless — and add the platform when a deal makes the
report a requirement and the observation window starts.
