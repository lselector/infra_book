# Clerk

An identity service whose distinguishing feature is
pre-built, styleable UI: sign-in, sign-up, user profile
and organisation management as components.

## When it is worth it

You want a polished account experience without designing
and building one. Organisation and team-membership support
is included, which is otherwise a meaningful chunk of work
for a B2B [[Multi-Tenant SaaS]].

## The trade

The UI convenience is tied to its component libraries and
supported frameworks. That is fine when you use them, and
less appealing on a plain server-rendered stack.
