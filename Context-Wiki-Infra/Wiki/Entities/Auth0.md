# Auth0

A mature identity platform covering social login,
enterprise SSO, SAML, custom rules and fine-grained
authorisation.

## When it is the right choice

- Customers require SAML or enterprise SSO — this is the
  usual trigger.
- You need custom logic during login (claims enrichment,
  step-up authentication, allow-lists).
- Compliance requirements benefit from its certifications
  and audit trail.

## When it is not

A small project with email and Google sign-in. The
capability is real and the price reflects it; per-active-
user costs escalate quickly past the free tier.
[[Firebase Authentication]] covers that case for
substantially less.
