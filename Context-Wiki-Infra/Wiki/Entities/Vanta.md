# Vanta

Connects to your cloud accounts, identity provider, code
host and HR system, monitors controls continuously, and
collects the evidence a [[SOC 2]] auditor will ask for.

## What you are buying

The evidence-gathering labour. Screenshots of MFA status,
lists of who has access, proof that encryption is on,
onboarding and offboarding records, policy
acknowledgements — collected automatically and refreshed,
rather than assembled by hand in the week before the
audit.

It also supplies policy templates, which removes another
chunk of the work described in
[[Trust Services Criteria]].

## The honest cost picture

Several thousand a year, on top of the audit fee. Worth it
when the alternative is a founder spending weeks on
evidence; less obviously worth it if you have one cloud
account and five employees.

## What it does not do

It does not perform the audit — you still engage a CPA
firm — and it does not implement controls. It tells you a
control is missing; fixing it is yours.
