# Cloudflare Registrar

Registers and renews domains at the wholesale registry
price plus ICANN fee, with no margin added.

## Why it is worth knowing

- Renewal pricing does not jump in year two, which is the
  standard practice elsewhere.
- WHOIS privacy is included, not sold separately.
- It is in the same account as [[Cloudflare DNS]] and
  [[Cloudflare Pages]], so a new site is one login.

## Constraints

- Cloudflare must be your DNS provider for the domain.
- Not every TLD is supported — check yours before
  planning a transfer.
- Domains cannot be transferred within 60 days of
  registration or a previous transfer, which is ICANN
  policy rather than Cloudflare's.

## Watch out for

Auto-renew and a valid payment method. A lapsed domain is
the fastest way to lose a business, and the recovery
window is short and expensive.
