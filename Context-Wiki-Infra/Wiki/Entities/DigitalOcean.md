# DigitalOcean

Droplets from $4-6/month, with a control panel and
tutorial library aimed squarely at developers rather than
enterprises.

## Why it appears here

The documentation. DigitalOcean's community tutorials are
frequently the clearest available explanation of a Linux
task, and that has real value when you are setting up your
first server.

## Practical notes

- One-click images for common stacks.
- Managed PostgreSQL available when you outgrow
  same-box hosting.
- Spaces for S3-compatible object storage.

## Watch out for

Managed database pricing starts well above the Droplet
price. Moving [[PostgreSQL]] off the box is a real cost
step — make it deliberately, on the signals in
[[Relational Databases]].
