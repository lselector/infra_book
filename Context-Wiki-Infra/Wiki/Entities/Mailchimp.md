# Mailchimp

Email marketing with landing pages, sign-up forms,
audiences and automations.

## Why it appears here

Two reasons. It is a direct alternative to [[AWeber]] at
rung 4 of [[The Ladder]], and its help documentation is
the clearest public explanation of the mechanics that
apply to every provider — [[Double Opt-In]], embedded
sign-up forms, and hosted landing pages.

## What you get

- Hosted landing pages, if you do not want to build one.
- Embeddable forms for a static site.
- Audience management with tags and segments.
- Automations for welcome and drip sequences.

## Watch out for

Pricing is per contact and rises steadily as the list
grows, including unengaged contacts. Prune inactive
subscribers — it lowers cost and improves
[[Email Deliverability]] at the same time.
