# Railway

A PaaS optimised for time-to-first-deploy: connect a
repository, and it infers the build and starts the
service.

## What it is good at

- Genuinely quick to a working URL, which makes it good
  for prototypes and internal tools.
- Usage-based pricing rather than fixed per-service fees.
- Databases provisioned in the same project with
  environment variables wired up automatically.

## Watch out for

Usage-based pricing means the bill scales with activity in
ways that are harder to predict than a flat monthly fee.
Set a spend limit — see [[Cost Control]].
